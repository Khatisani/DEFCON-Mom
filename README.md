# DEFCON: Mom

A simple calculator to tell you if you should ask mom for something right now or mind your business.

---

## Why this exists

My little sister has been acting up, but she still wants to ask for things. 

Walking up to mom blindly is a bad idea. If mom is already irritated or you messed up yesterday, asking to go out will just get you yelled at. You need data before you make a move. 

This tool looks at where you stand with mom right now and gives you a percentage chance so you know if it's safe to ask or if you should stay in your room.

---

## What it looks at (and why)

1. **When did she last catch you doing something wrong?**  
   If you got in trouble yesterday, the wound is fresh and her guard is up. If it's been a whole month of good behavior, she has probably forgotten and your chances are way better.

2. **How bad was the trouble?**  
   Forgetting to wash the dishes is easy to bounce back from. Sneaking out or getting caught in a serious lie takes weeks to recover from. Big mistakes carry big penalties.

3. **Is she actually over it?**  
   Sometimes time has passed, but she is still giving you the cold shoulder and sighing loudly when you walk into the kitchen. If she is back to sending memes on WhatsApp, you're in the clear. If she is giving one-word answers, do not ask for anything.



### Certain triggers bypass the math entirely:

 - 1. Asking for permission right as she walks through the front door from work.
- 2. An unwashed dish sitting in the sink while asking to go out.
- 3. Unfinished school work with a test the following morning.
